# Glider
A Free to play browser/ app game.

Take to the air in a high-stakes glider race where the wind is your ally and the terrain is your enemy. GLIDER puts you in the cockpit of a dynamic glider, challenging you to carve through treacherous mountain passes, floating sky ruins, and storm battered coastlines.

There is no need for engines here only pitch, roll, and the raw physics of flight. Use thermals to gain altitude, dive to build blistering speed, and risk a stall to trigger a recovery boost at dire times. Every gate you pass brings you closer to victory, but one wrong move means death.

The skies are waiting but the ground is closer than it seems.

## CONTROLS:

Keyboard (WASD Mode + Mouse):

> W / S – Pitch (nose down / nose up) Pull back to climb, push forward to dive and build speed.

> A / D – Roll (bank left / bank right) Tilt your wings to carve tight turns.

> Q / E – Rudder Yaw / Rudder Boost (yaw left / yaw right). Hold for 1s to charge an instant speed boost !

> Spacebar – Airbrake Deploy drag to shed speed instantly – use it to avoid overshooting gates.

> Esc – Pause / Resume Halt the action and tweak your settings and here you can change between inverse and inverse inverse.

> Move Mouse – Pitch / Roll  left/right and up /down of screen

> Q / E – Rudder Yaw (optional).

> Spacebar – Airbrake (if wanted)

> Esc – Pause / Resume


Controller:

> Left Stick – Pitch & Roll Push forward to dive, pull back to climb. Tilt left/right to bank.

> Right Stick (Horizontal) / L1 & R1 Bumpers – Rudder Yaw / Rudder Boost. Hold yaw to charge your rudder boost through coordinated turns!

> L2 / R2 Triggers – Airbrake Squeeze to slow down – Face buttons B or X also work as airbrake shortcuts.

> L1 / R1 Bumpers – Rudder Yaw (alternative)

> D-Pad – Pitch / Roll (Flight) & Navigation (Menus) 

> Start / Options – Pause / Resume

> A / Cross – Select / Confirm (Menus)

> B / Circle – Back / Cancel (Menus)


 Mobile?
Dont worry! You can use D-Pad, Analog, Touch anywhere on the screen OR Gyro!

## Installation Troubleshooting:

**macOS Users ("App is damaged and can't be opened"):**
macOS Gatekeeper blocks un signed apps by default (I dont have money). To fix this:
1. Drag `Glider.app` into your `/Applications` folder.
   
2. Open Terminal and run:

   xattr -cr /Applications/Glider.app

Open the app as normal!

**Windows Users ("Windows protected your PC"):**

  1. Click More info on the SmartScreen popup.

  2.Click Run anyway.



## How to Install for Offline Mobile Play 

*Glider* supports **Offline Play** by using Service Worker. You can install it directly to your phone's Home Screen without downloading it!

### iOS (iPhone / iPad)

> **Recommended App:** **Safari** *(Works on (iOS 16.4+) Chrome/Edge as well, but Safari gives the best experience)*.

1. Open **Safari** and go to one of the playable links:
   * [Play on Cloudflare](https://glider.pages.dev/)  - Im on the free plan, so if possible, use GitHub Pages
   * [Play on GitHub Pages](https://appishere.github.io/Glider/) - **(recommended)**
1. click 3 dots in bottom right
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square icon with an arrow pointing up) 
4. Scroll down the share menu and tap **Add to Home Screen**. (You may have to click View More)
5. Tap **Add** in the top right corner.
6. *Glider* will now appear on your home screen as a standalone app!



### Android

> **Recommended Apps:** **Google Chrome**, **Brave**, **Samsung Internet**, or **Firefox**.

1. Open **Google Chrome** (or your browser of choice) and go to:
   * [Play on Cloudflare](https://glider.pages.dev/) - Im on the free plan, so if possible, use GitHub Pages
   * [Play on GitHub Pages](https://appishere.github.io/Glider/) - **(recommended)**
2. Wait a few seconds for the page to load, then tap the **Three-Dot Menu** in the top-right corner.
3. Tap **Install App** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. Tap **Install** / **Add** when prompted.
5. The game app icon will appear in your App Drawer and on your Home Screen!
